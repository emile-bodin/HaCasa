Contract

AreaContractPreserved exception
Vertical orderBase n → s. The hero modifier (.card.hero) puts the big value first: s → n. One rule, set by the card with a class.Climate, plug and value cards (with a number) are hero. Media, climate and plug with a control row use a caption variant: the bottom line sits right under the status.
Bottom region.lbl is one row of --lh. Controls are absolute and anchored at --cp; the card reserves --reserve = control height + --bg.Appliance and action rows replace the bottom line; sockets use --bh-sock.
Side furniture--rr (right), --rl (left), --rc (corner text). Padding is --cp plus these.Climate rail, vertical slider, action column, sparkline, sub-content, media artwork panel.
Spacing sourceTokens on .card: --cp --lh --ib --bh --bh-sock --bh-media --bh-tile --ctl-w --vs-w --bg --gut. No card writes its own padding formula.Graph band tokens (--gl-*).
Responsive behaviorOne compact variant, cardCompact(all, compact), used under max-width:900px and for .strip.rows2. It is placed after every module's card rules.Media cards under 160px in two rows hide icon and corner; phones keep them.
Overlapsdev/layout-audit.mjs measures every region of every card at seven sizes: 0 overlaps and 0 overflows across 536 cards in seven layouts (before: 9 defects in 480).Corner text avoids the icon; in compact two rows it also moves left of an action column.
